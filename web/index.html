Tracing rays

How it works

SheepStealer Engine is a ray tracer: it follows rays of light from a camera through every pixel and works out what colour each one should be. Everything below runs on this server's processor, with no graphics card.

What the engine does

  • Spheres, planes, triangles, boxes, cylinders, cones and ellipsoids
  • Matte, mirror and glass materials, with reflection and refraction
  • Hard shadows, several lights, and depth of field
  • Anti-aliasing and multithreading, with identical output for any thread count
  • Scene files with line-numbered error messages and safety limits

Good to know

  • The prompt reader has a fixed vocabulary. It tells you which words it skipped.
  • The free server sleeps when nobody visits, so the first picture after a break can take about a minute.
