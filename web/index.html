Update 1.3: Directions

Latest23 November 2026

Prompt reader

  • Objects can now be placed relative to each other: to the right of, to the left of, beside / next to, above / over, on top of / on, under / below, in front of and behind.
  • Words like its, it and the work: a house with a tree to its right.
  • Counts work too: 3 bats above a house puts all three over the house.
  • The reader tells you what it understood, for example "cat (on top of table)".

Placement

  • Things on top of a table, chair or bench sit on its real surface. Things above float over the roof.
  • Under works for a table or bench: the object is shrunk to fit. Anything else goes in front instead, with a note.
  • Related objects use the same size as the one they are placed against. The camera and platform widen to fit.
  • The older words (left, right, background) still work as before.

Known issues

  • Big objects in front of small ones can hide them.
  • Two directions for one object (for example left of one thing and above another) use the first one.
Update 1.2: Block textures (23 November 2026)

Engine

Engine

  • New texture option for materials: grass, dirt, stone, sand, planks, water, lava, cloud, snow, leaves and bricks. Every block of space gets one flat colour, so surfaces look like pixel art.
  • New glow option, so lava and clouds light themselves.
  • Block size is set with scale. Textured scenes give the same picture on any number of threads.
  • Engine tests grew from 27 to 32, all passing.

New objects (8)

  • Grass block, stone block, lava block, water block, block tree, block cloud, block house and block sheep, in the new Blocks group. Total objects: 60.

Blocky look

  • Say blocky or pixel to draw the whole world in blocks: pixel grass, water, sand, snow, stone, dirt or lava ground and a flat blue sky.
  • A scene made only of block objects turns the blocky look on by itself.
  • New ground word: stone.

Textured ground and platforms

  • Ground words now draw real textures: lava ground, sand, water, snow, stone, cobblestone, dirt, planks, bricks.
  • Add a raised platform in the centre: a centre cobblestone ground on lava with a house on it, or a sand island in water.

Website

  • New update banner, pill and a new default picture. The Halloween theme stays.

Known issues

  • Block cloud and block sheep are drawn from simple boxes, so their faces are very plain.
  • Textures use a 3D block grid, so a sphere gets chunky blocks too.

How it works

SheepStealer Engine is a ray tracer: it follows rays of light from a camera through every pixel and works out what colour each one should be. Everything below runs on this server's processor, with no graphics card.

What the engine does

  • Spheres, planes, triangles, boxes, cylinders, cones, ellipsoids, tori and block textures
  • Matte, mirror and glass materials, with reflection and refraction
  • Hard shadows, several lights, and depth of field
  • Anti-aliasing and multithreading, with identical output for any thread count
  • Scene files with line-numbered error messages and safety limits

Good to know

  • The prompt reader has a fixed vocabulary. It tells you which words it skipped.
  • The free server sleeps when nobody visits, so the first picture after a break can take about a minute.
